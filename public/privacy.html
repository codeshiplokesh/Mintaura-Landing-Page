1. Introduction

Mintaura Bullion ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile application com.bulliongold.bullion and its associated digital marketplace. We are dedicated to respecting and safeguarding the privacy of bullion professionals, traders, refiners, mints, and raw material suppliers.

This policy details how data is collected, utilized, stored, protected, and purged in compliance with Google Play Developer policies, the Information Technology Act (India), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act).

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide secure B2B bullion marketplace functionality:

  • Personal & Contact Data: Name, business email address, phone number, operating city, and preferred communication channels.
  • B2B Verification Data: Business entity type (dealer, refiner, trader, etc.), monthly trade volume brackets, and voluntary business tax credentials (such as GSTIN) for verified status.
  • Device & Telemetry: Device model, OS version, Android ID, IP address, and application crash diagnostics.
  • Trading Inquiries: Communications and quote inquiries initiated between verified counterparties.

4. No Sale of Personal Data

Mintaura does NOT sell, rent, or trade personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies. Information is shared solely with verified trading partners when you explicitly initiate a business inquiry, or as required by law enforcement in accordance with Indian statutory requirements.

5. Account & Data Deletion Policy

How to Request Immediate Account & Data Deletion

Users may request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal and business data at any time. You do not need to have the app installed to submit a request.

To request deletion, send an email to mintaurabullion@gmail.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request - com.bulliongold.bullion along with your registered phone number or email.

Timeline: All personal details, business credentials, and communications will be irreversibly erased from our active databases within 30 days, and a written confirmation will be sent upon completion.

6. Security Safeguards

All data transmitted between the Android app and our cloud systems is protected by industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption. Storage repositories employ AES-256 encryption at rest with strict access control.

7. Children's Privacy

Our platform is exclusively intended for adult B2B bullion industry professionals. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from individuals under 18 years of age.
